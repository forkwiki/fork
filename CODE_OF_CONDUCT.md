# Code of conduct

FORK documents internet culture, including parts of it that are crude, political or hateful. Contributors are expected to do that work like archivists, not participants.

- Be respectful to other contributors. Critique data and code, not people.
- Describe objects neutrally and in your own words. Link to sources rather than reproducing them.
- Do not add private individuals, doxxing material, or content whose only purpose is harassment.
- Political lineages are welcome when they are documented; editorialising is not.
- Do not add follower counts, view counts or prices.

Maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions that break these rules. Report problems by opening an issue or contacting a maintainer privately.
